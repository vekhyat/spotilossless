Spotifast Lossless

An unofficial fork of Spotifast that plays Spotify Lossless through Spotify's Soloist receiver, on Linux. It is not made or supported by the Spotifast project.

Install

flatpak install --user https://vekhyat.github.io/spotilossless/spotifast-lossless.flatpakref

Updates then come with flatpak update or your software centre.

Then open Spotifast Lossless, sign in, and choose Settings > Playback > Audio quality > Lossless. You need Spotify Premium and your own Soloist key.

This address is a Flatpak repository: spotifast-lossless.flatpakrepo. Source, issues and releases are on GitHub.
