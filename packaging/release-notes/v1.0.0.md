Spotifast Lossless 1.0.0 is the first release of this unofficial Spotifast fork. On Linux it plays Spotify Lossless through Spotify's own Soloist receiver: choose Lossless in Settings and a guide sets it up, then Spotifast plays through it and shows the quality Spotify reports. It ships as a Flatpak that updates with `flatpak update`. It is built on Spotifast 0.14.1 and is not made or supported by the Spotifast project.

**Install:**

```sh
flatpak install --user https://vekhyat.github.io/spotilossless/spotifast-lossless.flatpakref
```

The `.flatpak` file below installs the same build, but does not update by itself.

## New

- **Play Spotify Lossless on Linux.** Choose Settings → Playback → Audio quality → Lossless. A guide downloads Soloist from Spotify, keeps your own Soloist key in your keyring, and waits for you to pair the receiver once from Spotify on your phone, with nothing else to install. If the phone doesn't list it, the guide shows the firewall command for the receiver's port, and how to close it again. After that, this computer's music plays through Soloist, shown as one device marked (this computer).
- **See the quality Spotify reports,** Lossless or Lossless 24-bit, in the player bar and the device picker, for whichever device is playing.
- **Offer lossless from the player bar,** on the device the device menu would list first among those that can play it.
- **Soloist looks after itself.** Spotifast fetches Spotify's current build 14 days before the installed one stops, or at once when Spotify stops it early, and keeps the pairing. If Soloist crashes or updates, it starts again at its last volume and carries on with the same song, from the same place, playing or paused.
- **The visualisers follow Soloist's sound,** captured from PipeWire. Soloist's sound skips Spotifast's equalizer.
- **Clear reasons when Soloist cannot run,** such as a missing `libatomic` or a glibc that is too old, a key the keyring refused, or a build for another processor.
- **A Flatpak of its own,** `io.github.vekhyat.SpotifastLossless`, from a signed repository. Soloist runs inside it against the Freedesktop runtime, so nothing needs installing on the system.
